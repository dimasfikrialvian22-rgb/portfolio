Project Summary

Designed an integrated risk management system for PDAM Surya Sembada, a municipal water utility company in Surabaya serving over 600,000 customers. This project integrates risk management into the performance management framework using the SOAR approach — ensuring every operational risk is directly linked to the company's strategic objectives, in compliance with ISO 31000 and the Indonesian Ministry of Public Works Regulation No. 20/PRT/M/2018.

The Problem & Proposed Solution

The Problem

  • Risk management system was not integrated with the company's performance management
  • 60% of companies fail to account for unforeseen risks (Allianz Risk Barometer 2020)
  • Risk identification was incomplete across all strategic objectives
  • No risk scorecard existed for visualization and monitoring purposes

Proposed Solution

  • An integrated RBPM system linking risks directly to strategic KPIs
  • SOAR framework: risk identification based on strategic objectives
  • Multi-dimensional risk assessment: operations, service, finance, and HR
  • Risk scorecard as a visual management dashboard for decision-making

Methodology — SOAR Framework

Deliverables

DELIVERABLE 01

Risk Register & Metrics

A comprehensive document mapping 82 risks with 145 risk metrics, classified by source using the 5M framework (Man, Money, Material, Machine, Method).

DELIVERABLE 02

Risk Assessment Matrix

Probability & impact evaluation across 4 business dimensions, producing risk rankings and risk maps in accordance with government regulation.

DELIVERABLE 03

Risk Control Plan

172 risk control actions with 215 control metrics, each with defined targets and measurement frequencies for ongoing effectiveness monitoring.

DELIVERABLE 04

Risk Response Strategy

Determination of risk treatment strategies — Tolerate, Transfer, Treat, or Terminate — based on scoring results for each Key Risk Indicator.

DELIVERABLE 05

Risk Scorecard

A comprehensive visual dashboard displaying all KPIs, associated risks, assessment scores, and risk responses in a management-friendly format.

DELIVERABLE 06

RBPM System Design

A ready-to-implement risk-based performance management framework that directly connects risk management activities to strategic business targets.
