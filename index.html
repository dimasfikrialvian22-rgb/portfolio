The Problem

  • PDAM Surya Sembada lacked an integrated risk system tied to strategic performance
  • Existing risk practices referenced ISO 31000 but were not connected to KPI achievement
  • No structured method to prioritize which risks posed the greatest threat to company objectives
  • Management had no visual tool for ongoing risk monitoring

My Approach

  • Applied SOAR (Strategy Objective at Risk) framework — linking each KPI to its risk landscape
  • Developed KRI for every KPI to enable early warning detection
  • Designed risk controls with measurable control metrics
  • Produced actionable risk response recommendations (Tolerate / Transfer / Treat / Terminate)
  • Delivered a Risk Scorecard as a practical management tool
